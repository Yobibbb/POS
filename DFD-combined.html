The CartAlogue POS is the sole central process. Three external entities define its system boundary.

The Smart Basket (ESP32) transmits barcode scan events and session data into the POS, and receives pairing acknowledgment and session confirmation in return. The Mobile Application sends pairing triggers and checkout requests, and receives live totals, checkout codes, and digital receipts. The Database supplies the product catalog and session records, and receives finalized transaction history and session state upon payment completion.

This combined Level 2 diagram shows all five POS processes decomposed into their simplified sub-steps within a single view. Each dashed purple group corresponds to one Level 1 process bubble. Sub-processes are numbered (e.g., 1.1, 1.2) and flow sequentially within each group (left to right internally). Data stores D1–D4 sit outside the groups and serve as the shared intermediaries between all process groups. External entities ESP32, Mobile App, and Database remain outside the system boundary throughout.

1.0 Session Management — Validates the pairing request, creates a session record in D2, and confirms the pairing to both the ESP32 and Mobile App.
2.0 Scan & Product Lookup — Receives each barcode from the ESP32, resolves it against D1, updates D2 with the new item, and broadcasts the running total to the Mobile App.
3.0 Checkout Processing — Receives the checkout request from the Mobile App, generates a unique code, writes it to D4, and delivers it as QR data back to the Mobile App.
4.0 Payment & Receipt — Retrieves the pending checkout from D4, computes the payment and change, and writes the completed transaction to D3 and marks D4 as done.
5.0 History Sync — Saves each transaction locally in D3, then pushes unsynced records to the external Database when connectivity is available.
